Fig. 1. Corpusil system architecture. A single request path forks once, at a
deterministic router, into three lanes that never rejoin. The fast lane returns
retrieved authority without invoking a language model; the deep lane runs a
five-agent LangGraph pipeline in which no claim is published unless the
verification agent maps it to a retrieved chunk. Dashed edges carry control
rather than data: the bounded retry (at most two) and the two abstention paths.
A = agent, S = service.
