## What's new

- **Parsi marriage questions now get the exact section.** Most of the Parsi Marriage and Divorce Act, 1936 had been read without its section numbers, because a sentence in section 6 that mentions "Schedule II" was mistaken for the start of a schedule. The Act now has all its sections, and Parsi questions go straight to the one that answers them: divorce (s.32), mutual consent (s.32B), judicial separation (s.34), a spouse who has left (s.36) and annulment (s.30).
- **The fix applies to every document.** Re-reading all 1,602 documents in the corpus with the corrected parser, the Parsi Act went from 6 sections to 55, two SEBI circulars gained sections, and nothing lost any.
- Builds on 0.4.4, which shows each community its own marriage law: Hindu, Christian, Muslim, Parsi, and the Special Marriage Act for court and interfaith marriages.

## Installing

The installers are not code-signed yet.

- **Windows:** if SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** if macOS will not open the app, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and press **Open Anyway**.

Already on 0.4.4 or earlier? Download and install this version over it. This release has no automatic update. If you connect to a shared Corpusil host, the improvements above come from the host, so they reach you as soon as it is updated.

Setting up and connecting to the host: see [RUN.md](https://github.com/DhruvReddyS/LEGAL_RAG/blob/main/RUN.md).
