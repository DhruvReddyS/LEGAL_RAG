## What's new

- **Divorce and separation questions get the law that applies to you.** India has a separate marriage law for each community, and their divorce sections read almost the same, so Corpusil sometimes answered a Hindu couple from the Special Marriage Act or a Christian couple from nothing at all. It now follows the community you mention: the Hindu Marriage Act when none is named, the Divorce Act, 1869 for Christians, the Dissolution of Muslim Marriages Act for Muslim wives, and the Special Marriage Act for court, civil and interfaith marriages.
- **Checked against questions we did not write.** On 117 real questions from Indian legal-advice forums (ILSIC, EACL 2026), the correct section is now shown for 42 instead of 19, and another community's law for 5 instead of 53. Our own 94-question set is unchanged.

## Installing

The installers are not code-signed yet.

- **Windows:** if SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** if macOS will not open the app, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and press **Open Anyway**.

Already on 0.4.3? Download and install this version over it. This release has no automatic update.

Setting up and connecting to the host: see [RUN.md](https://github.com/DhruvReddyS/LEGAL_RAG/blob/main/RUN.md).
